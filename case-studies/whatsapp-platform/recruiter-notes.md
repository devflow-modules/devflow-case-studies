# WhatsApp Platform Recruiter Notes

This document helps recruiters and technical interviewers understand what to evaluate in this case study.

---

## What This Case Demonstrates

The DevFlow WhatsApp Platform case demonstrates the ability to design and reason about a SaaS product with real business constraints.

It is not only a UI or CRUD project. It combines:

- Multi-tenant SaaS architecture
- WhatsApp Cloud API integration
- Webhook-based workflows
- Operational dashboards
- AI-assisted replies
- Role-based access control
- Usage tracking
- Stripe billing concepts
- White-label product thinking

---

### Frontend

### Backend

### Product Engineering

---

## Interview Discussion Topics

A technical interviewer could ask about:

- How tenant isolation is enforced
- How webhook duplication is avoided
- How AI replies are safely controlled
- How usage tracking supports billing
- How roles affect dashboard access
- How the product could scale to agencies and white-label clients
- How to test webhook and conversation flows
