# WhatsApp Platform Product Roadmap

This roadmap describes the product evolution path for the DevFlow WhatsApp Platform.

---

## Product Vision

Build a SaaS and white-label platform that turns WhatsApp operations into a structured customer communication workflow with inbox management, automation, AI assistance, usage tracking, billing and operational analytics.

---

## MVP Scope

### Core Account and Tenant Layer

- Tenant registration
- Tenant activation status
- User roles
- Protected routes
- Tenant-scoped data access

### WhatsApp Foundation

- WhatsApp channel registration
- Webhook configuration
- Inbound message handling
- Message persistence
- Basic conversation timeline

### Inbox Operations

- Conversation list
- Conversation details
- Assignment state
- Unanswered conversation indicators
- Basic operator workflow

### Management Dashboard

- Awaiting conversations
- Unassigned conversations
- Critical SLA threads
- Response indicators
- Channel activity

### Billing Foundation

- Plan model
- Usage counters
- Stripe customer/subscription concept
- Basic usage limits

---

## Phase 1 — Operational SaaS Foundation

Goal: make the product usable by a small business team.

- Login and tenant session
- WhatsApp webhook ingestion
- Inbox and conversation details
- Operator and manager roles
- Basic metrics dashboard
- Tenant activation controls

---

## Phase 2 — Automation and AI Assistance

Goal: increase operator productivity without removing human control.

- Suggested replies
- FAQ-based answer generation
- Conversation summarization
- AI safety checks
- Human assignment override
- Duplicate automation protection
- AI usage tracking

---

## Phase 3 — Billing and Plan Management

Goal: move from internal platform to monetizable SaaS.

- Stripe subscription integration
- Plan limits
- Usage aggregation
- Overage logic
- Billing status reconciliation
- Upgrade/downgrade flows

---

## Phase 4 — White-Label and Enterprise Readiness

Goal: support agencies, resellers and business clients.

- White-label tenant branding
- Custom onboarding links
- Custom domain strategy
- Advanced role permissions
- Tenant-level configuration
- Exportable reports

---

## Phase 5 — Advanced Operations

Goal: make the platform a serious operational layer.

- Sales funnel module
- SLA policies
- Conversation tags
- Team performance analytics
- Automation rules engine
- Multi-channel expansion
- Audit logs and compliance reports

---

## Future Monetization Paths

- Monthly SaaS subscriptions
- White-label licensing
- Setup fee for businesses
- Agency/reseller plan
- Usage-based AI add-on
- Premium analytics package
