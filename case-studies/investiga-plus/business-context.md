# Investiga+ Business Context

This document explains the business reasoning behind Investiga+.

---

## Market Context

Brazilian businesses frequently need to validate and analyze company information by CNPJ for commercial, operational and administrative workflows.

Common use cases include:

- Prospect validation
- Customer onboarding
- Supplier checking
- Sales qualification
- Internal registration
- Data cleanup
- Business analysis

---

## Business Problem

Many teams still perform company lookups manually across disconnected websites and spreadsheets.

This creates problems such as:

- Repetitive searches
- No centralized history
- No user-specific records
- No structured dashboard
- Repeated provider calls
- Hard-to-scale workflow
- Limited visibility into past analysis

---

## Product Opportunity

Investiga+ turns a repeated lookup workflow into a SaaS experience.

The opportunity is to centralize company consultation and gradually evolve into a business intelligence layer for company data.

---

## Target Users

### Sales and Prospecting Teams

Need to qualify companies before outreach or onboarding.

### Administrative Teams

Need to validate company data for registration and internal records.

### Consultants and Service Providers

Need quick access to company data during commercial or operational analysis.

### Small Businesses

Need a simple way to check company data without complex enterprise systems.

---

## Value Proposition

Investiga+ helps users:

- Validate company data faster
- Avoid repeated manual searches
- Keep search history
- Reuse cached results
- Access structured company information
- Build a more organized analysis workflow

---

## SaaS Potential

The product can evolve into:

- A paid company intelligence platform
- A lead validation tool
- A business data dashboard
- A white-label tool for consultants
- An API product for other systems

---

## Portfolio Value

This case demonstrates the ability to transform a real business operation into a SaaS product with authentication, API integration, persistence, caching, history and dashboard UX.
